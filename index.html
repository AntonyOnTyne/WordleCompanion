HOW TO USE

W*rdle Companion instructions

Use W*rdle Companion alongside your Wordle game. Enter your guesses and apply the colours you see to narrow down the possible answers.

Enter a guess

  • Type your 5ive-letter Wordle guess using your physical or the on-screen keyboard. Backspace removes the last letter.
  • Press Enter to submit. If the word isn’t in my dictionary, the row shakes and stays editable.

Match the clues

  • Tap the tiles to change their colour to match the colours of your Wordle guess.
  • Grey/yellow/green mean the same as in Wordle.

Narrow the list

  • The eliminated and remaining word counts update after each valid guess and every tile-colour change.
  • Choose Show words to view the remaining words alphabetically in a scrollable list. Tap a word to submit it as your next companion guess.
  • After five rows, tap a remaining word to copy it for your sixth Wordle guess.
